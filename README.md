# Lore

> *Tell it your day. It remembers your life.*

Lore is a voice-first AI journal for iOS and Android. You talk about your day, and Lore extracts the people, places, activities and feelings into moments. You can then browse your life by person, place or activity, relive it on a day-by-day Saga path, and ask it questions.

Website: [tellmylore.com](https://tellmylore.com)
